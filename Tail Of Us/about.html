About Us

Tails of Us is a veterinary hotel for pets who need more than ordinary boarding.

Every guest gets a comfortable room, daily care and a qualified team looking after them while you are away.

Veterinary Care & Facilities

Veterinary staff are on site every day to give medication, monitor health and respond quickly if a pet is unwell.

Our rooms have calm lighting, fresh bedding and easy-to-clean surfaces. Standard, Comfort and Suite rooms are available, with space to rest, play and recover.

You can share your pet's diet, medication and habits in the booking form, so the team can plan ahead.
