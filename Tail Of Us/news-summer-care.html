Summer Care Tips for Pets

Pet Care · 10 August 2026

Protect your pet from summer heat: offer plenty of fresh water, shaded areas with good airflow, and test hot pavement with your hand before stepping out for walks.

Heatstroke can progress rapidly. If your pet shows any signs of weakness, excessive drooling, or distress, seek immediate veterinary care.
